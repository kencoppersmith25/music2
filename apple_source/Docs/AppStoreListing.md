## Full Description
Experience the world’s music, engineered for the ride. 100% FREE.

No Hands Radio is the world’s most accessible radio app, specifically designed for drivers, bikers, and active users who need premium music without the distraction.

### Key Features
*   **Truly Hands-Free**: Fully integrated with Siri voice commands. Keep your hands on the wheel or bars and your eyes on the road. Simply say "Siri, Play", "Siri, Stop", "Siri, Next", or "Siri, Previous" to control stations and categories hands-free.
*   **A World of Music**: Access over 50,000 live stations from every corner of the globe. Explore curated genres ranging from Classical and Jazz to global favorites like Israeli, Indian, French, and Hawaiian radio.
*   **High-Contrast Interface**: Engineered with a "Midnight Black" background and ultra-high-visibility text. No Hands Radio ensures maximum readability under direct sunlight or during late-night rides.
*   **Smart Search History**: Your favorites are always at your fingertips. The app remembers your recent searches and genre history, creating quick-access buttons for instant navigation between your favorite categories.
*   **AirPlay Ready**: Take the journey home. Seamlessly beam your global music discoveries directly to your Smart TV or home theater system with one tap.
*   **Engineered for Stability**: Built for the long haul. Includes built-in signal monitoring features to ensure your stream stays smooth even when the road gets rough.

Discover the world’s music without ever taking your eyes off the road. Effortless, global, and truly hands-free.
